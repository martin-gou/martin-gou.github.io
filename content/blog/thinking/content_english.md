# A mistake I made

For a while, communicating in English was a major problem for me. I kept forcing myself to practice directly in English, but the result was not very good. Other people did not seem to have the same problem.

Later, I summarized the issue this way: Chinese is my mother language, so thinking in Chinese activates my brain faster. Once my thinking is activated, my English expression can also reach a much better level.
