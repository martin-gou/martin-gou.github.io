At different stages, my understanding of life changes.

# When I first entered university

When I first entered university, I often thought about this question. Maybe I was bored. Maybe I was attached to philosophical questions in the way many young people are attached to questions about the meaning of the world. At that time, my answer was simple: try to live happily every day. If happiness makes life feel comfortable, then a life filled with happy days sounds wonderful.

# Life as experience

Later, I began to feel that the meaning of life is experience. A life that is only happy may also become boring. I am like a child who wants to experience many things. I actively looked for my first internship. I explored ways to meet people online and practice English. I experienced deep relationships, studying abroad alone, jealousy, loneliness, and solitude. Some experiences were not happy when they happened, but after time passed, they became strange and meaningful memories.
